Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your MindHealth account and the personal data associated with it. MindHealth is a mental wellness app developed and operated by Oleg Sugonyaev.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to mind.health.app.res@gmail.com with the subject line “Account deletion request”.
  2. Include the email address you use to sign in to MindHealth, or mention the sign-in method linked to your account (Google, Yandex, or VK) so we can identify it.
  3. If possible, send the request from the email address linked to your account — this helps us verify that the request really comes from you.
  4. You will receive a reply confirming that your account and data have been deleted.

Your data is permanently deleted within 7 days of receiving your request. Deletion is irreversible and cannot be undone.

What data will be deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials, including linked Google, Yandex, and VK sign-ins;
  • your profile information;
  • all content synced from the app to our server: diary and daybook entries, mood records, thought records, AI chat conversations, and your progress in exercises and tests.

What is kept and for how long

  • Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within the same 7-day window;
  • anonymized usage statistics that can no longer be linked to you or your account are retained, since they do not identify you;
  • data stored only on your device (app settings and locally kept records) is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it from your device.

Questions

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at mind.health.app.res@gmail.com.
