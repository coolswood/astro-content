<h2>Privacy Policy (users outside the Russia region)</h2>
<p>Version dated 9 October 2026. This Policy describes what personal data the MindHealth service (the mobile app and related web resources) processes, on what legal basis, where the data is stored and what rights you have. It applies to users outside the Russia region; users in Russia are covered by a separate policy of that region's operator, available in the app.</p>

<h2>Who processes your data (controller)</h2>
<p>MindHelp OY, Business ID 3458080-8, Helsinki, Finland.</p>
<p>Contact for all matters and requests concerning personal data: <a href="mailto:mind.health.app.res@gmail.com">mind.health.app.res@gmail.com</a>.</p>

<h2>Our service principle: a closed processing loop</h2>
<p>The service is built as a closed loop of our own: your notes, conversations and audio are processed by our own servers and our own AI and speech-recognition models, hosted on our own infrastructure in the European Union.</p>
<p>We do not sell personal data, do not pass it to advertisers or data brokers, do not use advertising networks and do not show advertising based on your data.</p>
<p>In exceptional cases — when our own capacity is unavailable or a specific request technically cannot be fulfilled with our own means (for example, a very long audio recording) — we may engage an external technical service to process that request. The data is used only to fulfil that request.</p>

<h2>What data is processed</h2>
<p>The service is intended for users of the app. Depending on the features you use, the following are processed:</p>
<ul>
<li>account data: email address, device and app-install identifiers;</li>
<li>details you provide when setting up the service: name, gender, age, goals and requests;</li>
<li>the content of your work with the service: diary entries (text and photos), mood entries, exercise and test results, conversations with the AI assistant;</li>
<li>audio recordings you make for speech recognition;</li>
<li>support requests;</li>
<li>technical data: IP address, session and error-log details, push notification tokens;</li>
<li>payment information: subscription status and amount, payment history.</li>
</ul>
<p>Bank card details are handled by the app store; they are never transmitted to the service.</p>

<h2>Signing in</h2>
<p>You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or through a Google, Apple or Yandex account — in that case we receive your name and email address from it. Data received from Google is used in accordance with the Google API Services Limited Use requirements.</p>

<h2>Purposes and legal bases of processing</h2>
<ul>
<li>operation of the service: your account, syncing your notes across your devices, the AI assistant and speech recognition — performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR);</li>
<li>data about your psycho-emotional state (diary entries, mood entries, conversations with the AI assistant) — a special category of data (Art. 9 GDPR): processed on the basis of your consent, which you give by using the relevant features of the service and may withdraw at any time;</li>
<li>accepting payments and maintaining your subscription — performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b));</li>
<li>sending sign-in codes and push notifications — performance of the agreement (Art. 6(1)(b));</li>
<li>usage statistics and ensuring the stability and security of the service — the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)); you may object to such processing (Art. 21);</li>
<li>compliance with legal obligations — law (Art. 6(1)(c)).</li>
</ul>

<h2>Automated processing</h2>
<p>The AI assistant generates replies based on your notes and requests; speech recognition converts audio into text. These features carry out what you request; decisions producing legal effects concerning you are not made automatically.</p>

<h2>Where data is stored and for how long</h2>
<p>Data is stored on our own servers located in the European Union. Data is synced to the server if synchronisation is enabled; with synchronisation disabled, your notes remain only on your device.</p>
<p>Data is processed for as long as you use the service, unless a different period is established by law. When you delete your account, your diary entries, notes and user materials are deleted. Certain records — payment records, technical logs and the support correspondence archive — may be retained for the periods established by law or for as long as necessary for the service to operate; you may request the deletion of such data via the controller's contact.</p>

<h2>Who else has access to the data</h2>
<p>Besides us, limited access to the data is held only by those who keep the service running:</p>
<ul>
<li>app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) — to the extent necessary to process payments;</li>
<li>the email delivery service — to deliver sign-in codes;</li>
<li>push notification services (Google, Apple) — to deliver notifications;</li>
<li>product analytics services (hosted in the EU or on our own servers) — technical data: IP address, session details;</li>
<li>the hosting provider — to host and protect our servers (EU).</li>
</ul>
<p>These parties may not use the data for any other purposes. Data is never sold under any circumstances.</p>

<h2>Data transfers outside the EEA</h2>
<p>The main processing takes place in the European Union. Data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area only in the exceptional cases of engaging an external technical service described above — in compliance with the GDPR requirements for international data transfers (Chapter V) — and as part of platform functions: push notification delivery and payments are carried out by Google and Apple as the operators of those platforms.</p>

<h2>Security</h2>
<p>Data is transmitted over an encrypted connection. We apply the organisational and technical protection measures necessary to ensure the security of personal data and to protect it against unlawful access, destruction, alteration and disclosure.</p>

<h2>Age of users</h2>
<p>The service is intended for users aged 13 and over. In countries where the minimum age of consent to data processing is higher (up to 16), please use the service only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.</p>

<h2>Your rights</h2>
<p>Under the GDPR you have the right to: obtain confirmation that your data is being processed and information about that processing, as well as a copy of your data (Art. 15); rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16); request erasure (Art. 17); restrict processing (Art. 18); receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20); object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21); and withdraw your consent at any time — withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.</p>
<p>To exercise your rights, send a request to <a href="mailto:mind.health.app.res@gmail.com">mind.health.app.res@gmail.com</a>. We respond within one month (Art. 12(3) GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto, tietosuoja.fi).</p>

<h2>Changes to this Policy</h2>
<p>We may update this Policy. The current version is always available on this page, with its date shown in the header; we will additionally notify you in the app about material changes.</p>
